# Moon Technologies LLC — Entity Contributor Licence Agreement

> Draft pending legal review.

This agreement lets a company or other legal entity ("Entity") authorise its
employees and contractors to contribute to projects owned or managed by Moon
Technologies LLC ("Moon Technologies"), including The Moonlight Hotel.

1. The Entity grants Moon Technologies, for every Contribution submitted by its
   designated contributors, the same copyright and patent licences that sections
   2 and 3 of the Individual CLA ([CLA.md](CLA.md)) grant, on the same terms.

2. The Entity represents that it owns, or has the right to license, the
   intellectual property in those Contributions, and that each designated
   contributor is authorised to submit them on its behalf.

3. The Entity keeps a list of designated contributors (GitHub accounts) in its
   signature record, and will update it when people join or leave. Removing
   someone doesn't affect licences already granted.

4. Sections 5–7 of the Individual CLA apply to the Entity and its designated
   contributors.

To sign, an authorised officer emails legal@moonlighthotel.com with the
Entity's legal name, the signer's name and title, and the GitHub accounts to
designate. Moon Technologies records the signature in the `cla-signatures`
branch.
