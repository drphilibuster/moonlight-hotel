# Security

Please report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/drphilibuster/moonlight-hotel/security/advisories/new)
for this repository. Don't open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with
it. You'll get an acknowledgement within 3 working days.

In scope: everything in this repository and the hosted service at
moonlighthotel.com. Out of scope: denial of service by volume, social
engineering, and third-party services the hotel uses (report those to their
owners).

Don't access other people's accounts, rooms or media while testing, and don't
degrade the service for others. Good-faith research within these limits won't
be pursued by Moon Technologies LLC.
