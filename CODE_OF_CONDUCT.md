# Code of Conduct

This repository is part of The Moonlight Hotel, and the hotel's community rules
apply here as they do in its rooms: treat people with dignity, whatever their
identity, background or experience.

Not acceptable, in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions or any other
project space: harassment; sexual, pornographic, shock or gore content; hateful
or bigoted material; personal attacks; publishing others' private information;
and sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may block anyone who breaks
these rules, temporarily or permanently. Report problems to
conduct@moonlighthotel.com; reports are handled in confidence.
